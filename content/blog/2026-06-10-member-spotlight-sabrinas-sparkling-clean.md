## What Sabrina's Sparkling Clean does

Sabrina's Sparkling Clean is a residential cleaning business. In plain terms, Sabrina helps people come home to a clean house without spending their own evenings and weekends scrubbing it.

For a lot of West Valley households, that is not a luxury. It is how busy families, working couples and people with full schedules keep their homes under control. A good house cleaner gives people back time, and time is the one thing almost everyone wishes they had more of.

## Why a cleaner matters more in summer

June in the West Valley changes how people live in their homes:

- **Everyone is inside.** When it is 110° by mid-afternoon, families spend more time indoors, and floors, kitchens and bathrooms show it.
- **The kids are home.** School is out, and the house gets a lot more traffic during the week.
- **Dust season is starting.** Monsoon season is right around the corner, and anyone who has lived here through a dust storm knows how fast fine dust finds its way onto every surface.
- **People are moving.** Summer is a busy time for buying, selling and renting homes, and every move comes with a house that needs a deep clean on one end or the other.

A reliable residential cleaner takes one big recurring chore off the list during the most draining stretch of the year.
