One thing that makes Devoll Consulting stand out is its work with **owner-operators and small trucking companies**. Trucking comes with its own set of paperwork, including fuel tax reporting that has to be tracked carefully across the miles a truck travels. Having an accountant who understands how the industry works can save a trucking business a lot of time and stress.

If you know someone who drives their own rig or runs a small fleet, Tanya is a great person to introduce them to.

## Why midsummer is a smart time to call

July is a good moment for business owners to step back and look at the first half of the year. A few reasons to get organized now instead of waiting until next spring:

- **Midyear is a natural checkpoint.** Catching up on bookkeeping now makes year-end far less painful.
- **Estimated taxes do not wait.** Business owners who pay quarterly benefit from knowing where they stand.
- **Summer is often a little slower** for many local businesses, which makes it easier to find time to clean up the books.
- **Planning works best early.** Tax decisions are easier to make with months left in the year rather than weeks.

## Why it helps to have a CPA in your corner

Many small business owners start out doing their own books, and that can work for a while. As a business grows, though, the paperwork grows with it. Receipts pile up, categories get muddled and tax questions get harder to answer with a quick internet search. Working with a CPA means having someone who can keep the records clean, spot problems before they become expensive and explain what the numbers mean in plain terms. For a busy owner, that frees up time to focus on the work that actually brings in revenue.
