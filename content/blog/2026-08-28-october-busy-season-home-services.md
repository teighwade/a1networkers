It is the end of August in the West Valley, and most of us are counting down to the first morning that feels even a little cooler. For home service businesses, that countdown matters for another reason. When October arrives, homeowners come out of summer hibernation with a long list of projects, and the phones start ringing.

The businesses that handle the rush best are the ones that start preparing now.

## Why October gets so busy

Several things happen at once as the weather turns:

- **Outdoor work becomes bearable.** Painting, landscaping, roofing, pavers and pool projects that were put off all summer suddenly make sense again.
- **Monsoon damage gets noticed.** Storms can leave behind roof issues, fallen branches, damaged fences and drainage problems that homeowners finally have time to address.
- **Seasonal residents return.** Many part-time residents come back to homes that sat empty for months and need cleaning, maintenance or repairs.
- **The holidays are coming.** People want their homes ready for guests, which pushes cleaning, flooring, painting and handyman work up the list.
- **Heating systems wake up.** The first cool nights reveal which furnaces and heat pumps need attention.

## Get your schedule ready

A busy season is great, but only if you can keep up with it. A few things to do before October:

- **Look at your calendar realistically.** Know how many jobs you can handle per week without cutting corners.
- **Line up help.** If you rely on subcontractors or seasonal staff, confirm availability now.
- **Stock up on materials.** Supply delays can stall a busy season.
- **Set expectations.** Be honest about lead times when customers call. People appreciate a clear answer more than a promise you cannot keep.

## Tighten up your follow-up

When leads pour in, some inevitably slip through the cracks. Before the rush:

- Make sure every call, form and text goes to a place someone checks daily.
- Create simple templates for quotes and follow-up messages.
- Decide how quickly you will respond to new inquiries and stick to it.

A fast, professional response is often what separates the business that wins the job from the one that hears "we already hired someone."

## Reach out to past customers

Your best source of fall work may be people who already know you. Send a short note to past clients reminding them of seasonal services: a fall tune-up, a post-monsoon roof check, a deep clean before guests arrive. Repeat customers are easier to serve and are more likely to refer friends.

## Partner with other home service pros

Homeowners rarely need just one thing. Someone repairing monsoon damage on their roof might also need gutters, painting or a fence repair. A family getting ready for holiday guests might need carpet cleaning, a handyman and a fresh coat of paint.

This is where referral partnerships shine. When you have trusted relationships with other trades, you can:

- Recommend reliable professionals when a customer asks for something outside your specialty
- Receive referrals when those partners hear about work that fits you
- Coordinate on bigger projects so the homeowner has a smoother experience

Building those relationships before the rush means you will already know who to call when it hits.

## Use the next few weeks wisely

September is the calm before the storm. Use it to refresh your website, update your Google Business Profile with fall services, clean up your marketing materials and train your team. Small improvements now pay off when you are too busy to make them later.
