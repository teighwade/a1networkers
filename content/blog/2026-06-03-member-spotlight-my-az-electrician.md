## Why early summer is a good time to think about your electrical system

June in the West Valley means the AC is running nearly around the clock. Pool pumps, ceiling fans and second refrigerators in the garage all add to the load. For a lot of homes, especially older ones, this is the time of year when small electrical problems start to show up.

A few signs worth paying attention to as the heat builds:

- Breakers that trip when the AC kicks on
- Lights that dim or flicker when a large appliance starts
- Outlets or switch plates that feel warm to the touch
- A buzzing or burning smell near the panel

Monsoon season is also just around the corner. Storms bring wind, lightning and power outages, and it is a lot easier to have an electrician look at your panel or wiring now than during a stormy week in July. If you have been putting off that panel upgrade, a new circuit for an EV charger or extra outdoor lighting, early summer is a good window to get it scheduled.
