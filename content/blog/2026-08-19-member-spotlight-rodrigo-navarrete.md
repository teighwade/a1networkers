## Why August is a good time for an insurance review

Late summer brings two big reasons to look at your coverage:

- **Monsoon season is in full swing.** Wind, hail, falling trees and flash flooding can all damage homes and vehicles. It is worth knowing your deductible and what your policy actually covers before the next storm, not after.
- **School is back in session.** For a lot of families, that means a teen driver is on the road for the first time, or a student is heading off to college. Both are moments when coverage needs often change.

Add in the people who bought or rented a home over the summer, and August is a busy month for insurance questions.
