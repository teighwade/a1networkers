Every chapter of BNI has one seat per profession, and at BNI A1 Networkers the heating and air conditioning seat belongs to **Pro Solutions Air Conditioning & Heating**, represented in our chapter by **Jordan Harford**.

## A family-owned West Valley business

Pro Solutions Air is a family-owned company based in Surprise, Arizona, serving the West Valley since 2011. They are licensed, bonded and insured (ROC #285896), and their motto says it all: *Never a problem, just a solution.*

## What they do

Pro Solutions Air takes care of the systems that keep Arizona homes livable all year:

- **AC repair**, including same-day service when it is available
- **New AC installation and replacement**, including split systems, package units, ductless mini-splits and high-efficiency inverter systems
- **Heating repair** for heat pumps and furnaces as the nights cool down
- **Tune-ups and maintenance memberships** to catch small problems before they become big ones
- **Indoor air quality**, including air purification options

## Helpful tools for homeowners

One thing that sets Pro Solutions Air apart is how much they help homeowners before they ever pick up the phone. Their website includes free tools like a new AC price estimator, a repair-or-replace calculator, an AC size calculator, a troubleshooter for common AC and heater problems and a Home Comfort Score. If you have ever wondered whether your system is worth repairing, those tools are a great place to start.

## Why fall is the right time to call

Now that the summer heat has broken, fall is the perfect time to have a heating system checked before the first cold night. It is also the best time to plan an AC replacement, because the schedule is lighter than in July and you will not be making a big decision during a 115° heat wave.

## How you can refer Pro Solutions Air

A great referral for Pro Solutions Air is:

- A homeowner whose AC or heat pump is more than 12–15 years old
- Someone who mentions a high electric bill or rooms that never get comfortable
- A new homeowner who does not know when the system was last serviced
- Anyone who just had an expensive repair and is wondering what to do next

If someone you know fits, connect them with Jordan or point them to [prosolutionsair.com](https://prosolutionsair.com/).
