For most families, a car is one of the most important tools they own. Having a mechanic you trust is one of those things you do not appreciate until you need one.

## Why late September is a good time for a check-up

Arizona summers are tough on vehicles. After months of triple-digit heat, it is worth having a professional take a look before fall road trips and the busy holiday season. A few things the heat is especially hard on:

- **Batteries.** Heat shortens battery life, and many batteries that limped through summer fail when the weather changes.
- **Cooling systems.** Radiators, hoses and coolant work overtime from June through September.
- **Tires.** Hot pavement wears tires faster and can expose weak spots.
- **AC.** After a summer of running nonstop, plenty of car AC systems are not blowing as cold as they used to.
- **Fluids and belts.** Heat breaks down fluids and dries out rubber parts.

Fall is also when a lot of people start planning drives to see family, head up north for cooler weather or travel for the holidays. Getting the car checked now beats dealing with a breakdown on the I-17.

## Why a trusted mechanic is a great referral

Auto repair is one of those industries where people really want a personal recommendation. Most drivers do not know how to judge a repair quote, so they rely on someone they trust to point them to a shop. That is exactly what a referral network is for.

## How you can refer PBS Automotive

A great referral for Robert is someone who:

- Has a check engine light on and has been ignoring it
- Is planning a road trip this fall or over the holidays
- Just bought a used car and wants it checked over
- Has a teen driver with a new-to-them vehicle
- Is frustrated with their current shop or does not have a mechanic they trust
- Noticed their car AC struggled this summer
- Owns a small business with work vehicles that need regular maintenance

Listen for phrases like "my car is making a weird noise" or "I don't know who to take it to." Those are perfect moments to make an introduction.
