## Why summer matters for plumbing

Late June in the Valley is hard on a house. A few things plumbers commonly deal with this time of year:

- **Water heaters under strain.** Hard water leaves mineral buildup in tanks over time, and many older heaters reach the end of their life without much warning.
- **Pipes in hot attics and garages.** Lines that run through very hot spaces can be more prone to problems.
- **Outdoor plumbing.** Irrigation, hose bibs and pool equipment get heavy use when it is 110° outside.
- **Monsoon season.** As storms roll in, drains and yard drainage get tested.

A plumbing problem in summer is not just an inconvenience. Water damage can spread quickly, and nobody wants to go without hot water or a working bathroom for long. Having a plumber you trust before something breaks makes a big difference.

## Hard water and tankless options

If you have lived in the West Valley for any length of time, you know about hard water. Scale builds up in fixtures, water heaters and appliances. Rapid Fire Plumbing works with tankless water heaters and offers descaling service for them, and they install water softeners for homeowners who want to cut down on buildup. If someone you know keeps replacing fixtures or complains about white crust on everything, that is a good conversation to start.
