## Why late summer is a good time to clean

By early September, West Valley homes have been through a long summer. Monsoon storms track dust and mud indoors, kids and pets spend more time inside to escape the heat and doors open and close all day with people coming and going. All of that shows up in carpets, rugs and grout lines.

It is also a good time to plan ahead. Cooler weather is coming, and many families start thinking about hosting guests in the fall. Getting carpets, furniture and tile cleaned before the holiday calendar fills up means one less thing to worry about later.

## Tile and grout deserve attention too

Tile is everywhere in Arizona homes, and grout is one of the hardest things to keep looking clean. It is porous, so it soaks up dirt and spills over time. Regular mopping often just moves the dirt into the grout lines. Professional tile and grout cleaning can make a floor look dramatically better without replacing anything, which is a good message for homeowners who think their only option is a remodel.
