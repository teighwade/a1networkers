## Why the holidays are hard on a home's wiring

Most of the year, a home's electrical system does the same job day after day. The holidays change that. A few things tend to pile up at once:

- **Outdoor lighting and decorations** plugged into exterior outlets that may not have been tested in a year
- **Extension cords and power strips** chained together to reach the tree or the front yard
- **Big cooking days** with ovens, roasters, slow cookers and coffee makers all running together
- **Space heaters** coming out of the closet as Arizona nights finally cool down
- **More people in the house**, which means more devices, more lamps and more load

None of that is a problem in a healthy system. But it is the kind of extra demand that tends to expose small issues that were already there.

## Signs it is time to call an electrician

If you notice any of these while you are decorating or hosting, it is worth having a professional take a look rather than just resetting a breaker and hoping for the best:

- A breaker that trips every time a certain combination of things is running
- Outdoor outlets that stop working or trip when it is damp out
- Lights that dim or flicker when an appliance kicks on
- Outlets, cords or switch plates that feel warm
- A buzzing sound or burning smell near an outlet or the panel

Getting these checked in November, before the busiest weeks, is a lot easier than chasing an emergency on a holiday weekend. It is also a good time to think about projects people often put off, like adding a dedicated circuit, updating lighting or upgrading an older panel before a remodel next year.
