In a BNI chapter, every seat represents one profession, and at BNI A1 Networkers the promotional products seat belongs to **Logo Loco LLC**, represented by **Teigh Wade**.

If you have ever handed out a branded pen, worn a company T-shirt to an event or grabbed a free water bottle at a booth, you already know what promotional products can do. Teigh's job is to help businesses choose the right ones and get them done well.

## What Logo Loco does

Logo Loco is a woman-owned business based in the West Valley that helps companies promote themselves with **high-quality branded merchandise**. In other words, they help you put your logo on things people will actually keep and use.

Promotional products cover a wide range. Depending on the goal, a business might be looking for:

- **Apparel** for staff, teams or event volunteers
- **Giveaways** for trade shows, open houses and community events
- **Client and employee gifts** that say thank you
- **Everyday items** that keep your name on a desk, in a car or in a kitchen

The hard part for most business owners is not finding a product. It is choosing something that fits the audience, the budget and the timeline, and then making sure the logo looks right when it arrives. That is where having a promotional products specialist in your corner helps.

## Why branded merchandise still works

Digital ads disappear the moment someone scrolls past. A useful item with your name on it can sit in front of a customer for months. For local service businesses in particular, that kind of quiet, repeated reminder can be the reason someone calls you instead of a competitor.

Branded merchandise also helps businesses look established and organized. A crew in matching shirts, a real estate agent with a thoughtful closing gift or a booth with good giveaways all send the same message: this company takes its brand seriously.

## Why June is the time to plan ahead

It is hot in the West Valley right now, and for many businesses the summer is a little slower. That makes it a smart time to plan for the busier months ahead:

- **Fall events and festivals** come back once the weather cools down.
- **Back-to-school** brings school, sports and club orders.
- **Trade shows and expos** pick up in the fall.
- **Holiday gifts** for clients and employees go much more smoothly when they are ordered early, not in a December rush.

Custom items take time to design, approve and produce. Starting the conversation in summer gives you more choices and less stress.

## How you can refer Logo Loco

A great referral for Teigh is:

- A business owner preparing for a trade show, expo or community event
- A company that needs uniforms or branded apparel for its team
- A real estate agent, lender or other professional who gives closing or thank-you gifts
- A school, sports league, church or nonprofit planning a fundraiser or season
- A business that just opened, rebranded or is celebrating a milestone
- An HR manager or office manager planning employee appreciation gifts

A simple way to start the conversation: *"Have you thought about what you are handing out at your next event?"* If the answer is "not yet," connect them with Teigh or point them to [logoloco.co](https://logoloco.co).
