- **Emergency service** when a roof problem cannot wait

## Why September is the time for a roof check

Monsoon season in the Valley is winding down, and it can be hard on roofs. Strong winds, heavy bursts of rain and blowing debris can crack or shift tiles, lift shingles and expose underlayment. Many of these problems are not obvious from the ground, and a small leak can go unnoticed until it causes damage inside the home.

A few reasons to schedule an inspection now:

- **Catch storm damage early.** A small repair today is easier and less costly than water damage later.
- **Get ahead of winter rain.** Arizona does not get much winter rain, but when it comes, a damaged roof will let you know.
- **Beat the fall rush.** As temperatures drop, homeowners start tackling project lists, and contractor schedules fill up.
- **Prepare for buying or selling.** A roof in good shape gives buyers and sellers one less thing to worry about.

Tile roofs deserve special mention. The tiles themselves often last a long time, but the underlayment beneath them wears out with years of Arizona sun. Having a professional look at it periodically is a smart move for any homeowner with an older tile roof.

## A process built around communication

Boost Roofing puts a strong emphasis on keeping homeowners informed. Their process starts with an inspection and documentation of the roof, followed by a photo-based breakdown of the options so the homeowner can see what is going on up there instead of taking it on faith. Customers get progress updates during the job, and the work wraps up with a cleanup and completion photos. For a homeowner who has never had roof work done, that kind of transparency makes a big decision feel a lot more manageable.
