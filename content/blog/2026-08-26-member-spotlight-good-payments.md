## Why processing is worth a second look

Card processing is one of those costs many owners set up once and never revisit. Over time, rates, fees and equipment needs can change, and a setup that made sense a few years ago may not be the best fit today.

Late summer is a smart time to take a look. The fourth quarter is the busiest season for many retailers, restaurants and service businesses. Making sure your payment setup is efficient, secure and working smoothly **before** the holiday rush is much easier than trying to fix something in December.

A review can also uncover opportunities beyond rates, like connecting a POS system to accounting software or adding tools that help bring customers back.
