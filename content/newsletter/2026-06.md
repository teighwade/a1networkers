## Seasonal tips for the West Valley

**At home**

- **Give your AC some help.** Change the air filter, keep the outdoor unit clear of weeds and debris, and close blinds on west-facing windows in the afternoon.
- **Use ceiling fans the smart way.** Set them to spin counterclockwise in summer so they push air down, and turn them off when you leave the room. Fans cool people, not rooms.
- **Keep the pool safe.** Check that gates latch on their own, keep a phone and a flotation device poolside, and always have an adult watching kids in the water.
- **Look at outdoor outlets.** Pool equipment and patio outlets should be on GFCI protection. If a GFCI keeps tripping, have it looked at instead of resetting it over and over.
- **Get ready for monsoon.** Monsoon season officially starts June 15. Trim trees near the house, secure patio furniture and think about surge protection for TVs and computers.
- **Never leave kids or pets in a parked car,** not even for a minute.

**For your business**

- **Protect your people from the heat.** If you have crews working outside, plan the hardest work for early morning, build in shade and water breaks, and know the signs of heat illness.
- **Do a mid-year check-in.** June closes out the first half of the year. Compare where you are with your goals and adjust your plan for the second half.
- **Plan for summer slowdowns.** If your business gets quieter in summer, use the time to update your website, clean up your client list and book one-to-ones.
- **Have an outage plan.** Know what you will do if the power goes out during a storm: backups for important files, a way to reach customers and a list of who to call.

## Networking tip of the month: book your summer one-to-ones

Summer is the best time of year to schedule one-to-ones. Calendars are a little lighter, and a coffee in an air-conditioned spot is a welcome break from the heat. Set a goal to meet with two or three members you do not know well yet. Ask what a great client looks like for them, what they wish more people knew about their business, and how you can spot a good referral for them. You will come out of the summer with a much clearer picture of who to send their way, and they will know a lot more about you.
