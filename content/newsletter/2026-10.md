## Welcome to October

We made it. October is the month West Valley residents wait for all summer. The mornings finally cool off, the windows can open again and patios fill back up. Fall festivals, Halloween and the start of the holiday season are just around the corner.

It is also one of the best months of the year for business. People are back from summer travel, ready to start projects before the holidays, and planning for the new year. If you have been waiting for the right time to step up your networking, this is it.

## Seasonal tips for the West Valley

**At home**

- **Test your heat early.** Run your heater or heat pump on the first cool morning. A brief dusty smell is normal. Strange noises or no heat means it is time for a checkup.
- **Get the yard ready for fall.** October is a popular time to overseed lawns, plant winter vegetables and refresh flower beds now that the heat has broken.
- **Have a safe Halloween.** Halloween is Saturday, October 31. Use flashlights or glow sticks, check treats before kids dig in and drive slowly through neighborhoods that evening.
- **Protect your devices at home.** Update your phones and computers, use strong passwords and turn on two-step sign-in for email and banking.
- **Enjoy fall events.** Fairs, festivals and pumpkin patches pop up around the Valley this time of year. Get out and enjoy the weather.

**For your business**

- **Review your benefits.** For many businesses and families, fall is open enrollment season for health insurance and benefits. Medicare open enrollment runs October 15 through December 7. Now is a good time to talk with your insurance agent.
- **Do a cybersecurity check.** Make sure software is up to date, backups are working and staff know how to spot a phishing email.
- **Prepare for the holiday rush.** Plan your staffing, inventory and marketing for November and December now.
- **Lock in year-end projects.** Customers who want work done before the holidays are deciding now. Reach out and get on their calendars.

## Networking tip of the month: follow up within two days

When you meet someone new, whether it is a visitor at our chapter or a contact at a fall event, follow up within a day or two. Mention something specific you talked about, and if you promised an introduction or a resource, send it right away. Quick, thoughtful follow-up tells people you are reliable, and reliable people are the ones who get referred.

## BNI news: build a network that refers you

BNI Global's blog recently laid out how referral relationships develop. First you build a real relationship. Then you help the other person understand what you do, simply enough that they can repeat it. Next you give them a reason to want to help you, through appreciation and consistent contact. Only then do you ask for referrals. The article also makes a useful point: if referrals are not coming in, it is often because people do not fully understand your business or do not yet trust you enough. Both can be fixed with time and clear communication.
