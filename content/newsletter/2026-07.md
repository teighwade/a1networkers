## Seasonal tips for the West Valley

**At home**

- **Celebrate the Fourth safely.** Fireworks rules differ from city to city, so check your city's rules before you light anything. Keep a hose or bucket of water nearby, and never let kids handle fireworks on their own.
- **Watch your pets.** Fireworks scare a lot of dogs. Keep them inside with the TV or radio on, and make sure their tags and microchip information are up to date.
- **Prepare for monsoon storms.** Trim dead branches, clear gutters and roof drains, and bring in or tie down anything the wind could pick up.
- **Never drive into a flooded wash.** It only takes a little moving water to sweep a car away. Turn around and find another route.
- **Keep a storm kit handy.** Flashlights, batteries, a charged power bank and water can make a power outage a lot less stressful.
- **Check for leaks after storms.** Look at ceilings, window frames and door thresholds after a heavy rain so small leaks do not become big repairs.

**For your business**

- **Plan around the holiday.** The Fourth falls on a Saturday this year, so many offices will observe it on Friday, July 3. Let customers know your hours ahead of time.
- **Set your second-half goals.** Look at what worked in the first six months and pick one or two things to focus on through December.
- **Back up your data.** Monsoon storms can knock out power without warning. Make sure important files are backed up somewhere other than the computer on your desk.
- **Keep crews safe in the heat.** Start outdoor work early, provide shade and plenty of water, and keep an eye on each other.

## Networking tip of the month: be a great host

Every visitor who walks through our door is deciding whether this is a room they want to be part of. You do not have to be an official Visitor Host to make them feel welcome. Introduce yourself, ask what they do and who their ideal client is, and if you know someone who might be a good contact for them, say so. Then follow up after the meeting. A short note or a quick call tells a visitor that this group really does look out for each other.

## BNI news: referrals start with relationships

A core idea in BNI is that referrals are not just about who you know. They come from relationships where people know you, like you and trust you. That trust takes time, and it grows through small things: showing up on time, keeping your word, and taking an interest in other members' businesses. When you focus on the relationship first, referrals tend to follow.
