## Welcome to August

School is back in session across the West Valley, morning traffic is picking up again, and the monsoon is in full swing. For a lot of families and businesses, August feels like a reset. Summer schedules wind down, routines come back and people start thinking about the rest of the year.

That makes August a great month to recommit to your networking. Look at your calendar, look at the referrals you have given and received this year, and decide what you want the next five months to look like. A little planning now goes a long way by December.

## Seasonal tips for the West Valley

**At home**

- **Slow down in school zones.** Kids are back on the sidewalks and crosswalks early in the morning. Leave a few extra minutes and watch for buses.
- **Pull aside, stay alive.** If you are driving into a dust storm, pull off the road as far as you safely can, turn off your lights, take your foot off the brake and wait it out.
- **Make a family routine.** A shared calendar, a set place for backpacks and a plan for after-school pickup make busy mornings easier.
- **Watch for scorpions and pests.** Summer heat and storms push them indoors. Seal gaps around doors and keep a black light handy for a quick nighttime check.
- **Check your yard after storms.** Look for leaning trees, damaged fences and debris in drains, and clean them up before the next storm.

**For your business**

- **Get your books current.** Reconcile your accounts through July so you know where you stand heading into the fourth quarter.
- **Plan for estimated taxes.** If you pay quarterly estimates, set aside money now for the September payment.
- **Reach out to families.** If your business serves parents, back-to-school season is a natural time to let them know how you can help.
- **Start thinking about Q4.** Fall and the holidays come quickly. Sketch out your plans for promotions, staffing and year-end deadlines now.

## Networking tip of the month: give a referral before you ask for one

Before your next weekly presentation, ask yourself: have I given anyone in this room a referral lately? The quickest way to get more referrals is to become known as someone who gives them. Listen closely to each member's ask, write down one or two names that come to mind, and follow up that week. Even a small referral, like an introduction to a neighbor or a vendor you trust, shows the room you are paying attention.

## BNI news: know your referral partners

BNI often talks about building a "contact sphere," a group of businesses that serve the same kinds of clients without competing with each other. A real estate agent, a lender, a home inspector and a title officer all work with home buyers, for example. An accountant, an attorney, a banker and an insurance agent all work with business owners. When members in the same contact sphere get to know each other well, they can pass referrals back and forth naturally. Take a look at the room and ask who is in yours.
